01 / From request to receipt

A capable agent.
A controlled payment path.

A fluent explanation is not authorization. AgentVault checks trusted records and policy before allowing a simulated payment.

01 — INVESTIGATE

Follow the evidence.

Compare invoice details, sender identity, and bank destinations with trusted fixture records. MoneyTrace shows where a decision came from.

02 — AUTHORIZE

Give policy the final say.

Check spending limits and risk signals. Reserve budget for authorized payments, block conflicts, and route ambiguity to a person.

03 — EXECUTE

Act within the boundary.

Payment tools require stored authorization. Repeated settlement is idempotent; human review remains separate from the agent.

02 / Privacy is part of the trace

Useful context.
Fewer exposed details.

The MCP guard masks sensitive fields before tool results reach the model. A disclosure ledger records sanitized previews and detected redactions so you can inspect what crossed that boundary.

The default guard is rule-based. An optional offline OpenAI privacy-filter adapter is included; its model is not loaded by default.
